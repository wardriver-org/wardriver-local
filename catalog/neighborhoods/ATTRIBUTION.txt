MAJOR U.S. CITY NEIGHBORHOOD CATALOG

City eligibility and activation geometry: U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census
TIGERweb incorporated places >=250,000 residents, plus Urban Honolulu CDP.
50 states and District of Columbia only. City geometries are generalized to
0.001 degree from the source service; this is not a legal boundary dataset.
https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Census2020/tigerWMS_Census2020/MapServer

Zillow neighborhood data: © Zillow, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Sources: AlexMotz/us-neighborhood-geojson, tree
 a1fe3bb6cd4cb54b7ccfd6cdbe782c55d1db862f
and US EPA Zillow Neighborhoods (2017).
https://gispub.epa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/OEI/Zillow_Neighborhoods/MapServer
https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/neighborhoods-us-2017-zillow-segs
Changes: selected major cities, normalized metadata, rounded coordinates to
five decimal places, deduplicated names, and split into compressed city packs.
Zillow-derived records in these packs remain under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Additional mapped places for sparse cities: © OpenStreetMap contributors,
Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL).
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
These OSM-derived records and their derived survey boxes are available under
ODbL 1.0. Points are represented by approximate boxes extending 500 meters
north/south/east/west from the mapped place center; bounded features use their
bounding box. These are survey targets, not asserted neighborhood boundaries.
Source element URLs and queries are retained in the packs. Source snapshot
retrieved 2026-09-24.

Laredo: City of Laredo Open Data, OL Neighborhoods. These are unofficial
neighborhoods of Old Laredo. See LICENSE.laredo.txt for the source disclaimer.

Los Angeles: original Wardriver legacy survey boxes restored with original
keys. They are approximate progress regions, including nearby communities.

Each city pack contains source and license metadata. The index is an inventory
of independently attributed data sources, not a claim that all records share
one license. Source data licenses do not assign a license to application code.
No source organization endorses Wardriver. Coverage and vintage vary by city;
this catalog is not an exhaustive or official list of neighborhoods.
